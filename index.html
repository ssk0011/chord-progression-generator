    Harmonize a melody

    Put | between bars and each bar’s notes are fitted in. The chords follow the chart’s key and time, and .

    Note names like C, F♯ or F#, B♭ or Bb. A dash (-) holds the last note and a dot (.) is a rest. With | between bars, each bar’s notes are fitted into the bar, however many there are: extra notes become two to a beat (eighth notes in 4/4), on the weaker beats first. Put two notes in one beat yourself with brackets: (A B). Without bars, it is one note per beat. Octaves are ignored; notes are matched by pitch.

    Edit as text
    Write it like a chart: one line per phrase, | between bars, chords sharing a bar split it. Start a line with [Verse] or Chorus: to label a section. Add :2 for an exact beat count, ? for a slot you want ideas for. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter loads. Drag chords in the chart below to reorder them (hold Option/Alt to copy instead: dropped on a chord or blank, the copy fills it), drag a section’s name or its Song form chip to move the whole section, or drag a chord’s right edge to change its length (hold Shift to trade beats with the next chord); the text updates to match.
